# BusinessAI Specification v0.1

Status: Future Phase
Priority: Phase 2

---

# 1. Overview

BusinessAI is the operational intelligence layer
that manages the business lifecycle surrounding products.

While ProductAI focuses on:

```text
Building Products
```

BusinessAI focuses on:

```text
Operating Businesses
```

---

# 2. Relationship with ProductAI

```text
ProductAI
    ↕
Operational Interface
    ↕
BusinessAI
```

---

## ProductAI Responsibilities

- software development
- mission execution
- AI worker orchestration
- memory
- reflection
- GitHub workflows
- operational improvements

---

## BusinessAI Responsibilities

- KPI management
- revenue tracking
- customer operations
- CRM
- growth analysis
- support operations
- business strategy
- analytics

---

# 3. Philosophy

## Products Are Not Enough

Building software alone is insufficient.

Long-term success requires:

- operation
- customer understanding
- sustainable workflows
- business intelligence
- organizational continuity

---

## Human-centered Operations

BusinessAI should support humans,
not replace strategic human judgment.

Humans define:
- vision
- ethics
- business direction
- company culture

AI supports:
- organization
- analysis
- optimization
- continuity

---

# 4. Operational Intelligence

BusinessAI should continuously monitor:

- customer feedback
- operational trends
- KPI changes
- product health
- support load
- growth indicators

---

## Goal

Transform operational information into:
- actionable insights
- organizational learning
- sustainable growth

# 6. Relationship with Memory

BusinessAI should connect to:

```text
ProductAI Memory System
```

through structured operational interfaces.

---

## Shared Knowledge

Potential shared knowledge:
- operational incidents
- customer pain points
- repeated failures
- growth learnings
- workflow insights

---

## Boundary Philosophy

BusinessAI should NOT directly control:
- product implementation
- worker execution
- repository workflows

Those remain ProductAI responsibilities.

```text
ExecutiveAI
      ↑
BusinessAI

ProductAI
```

---

## ProductAI

Builds products.

---

## BusinessAI

---

## ExecutiveAI

---

# 9. Current Scope

BusinessAI is NOT part of ProductAI Phase 1.

Current ProductAI implementation should only:
- prepare future integration points
- maintain operational interfaces
- preserve memory compatibility

---

# 10. Long-term Vision

BusinessAI aims to become:

An AI-supported business operations layer
that helps individuals and small organizations
manage software businesses with operational intelligence,
organizational memory, and continuous improvement.
